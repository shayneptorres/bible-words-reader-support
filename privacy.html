Privacy Policy

Bible Words Reader · Effective September 25, 2026

Support home Common questions

The short version

Bible Words Reader collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Everything you create in the app stays on your iPhone. This matches the app's App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Your vocabulary lists, flashcard progress, reading plans, reading history and streak, daily goal, notes, bookmarks and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone. I (the developer) can't see them, and they aren't sent anywhere.

  • There is no account and no cloud sync.
  • Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup, or a backup to your computer) includes this data, as it does for most apps. Those backups are under your control and governed by Apple.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

No network use for app features

The Bible text and word data are bundled with the app, and the app makes no network requests to provide its features. If you choose to open a passage or word in another app or website — such as YouVersion, BibleGateway or Logos — that app or site opens with the reference you picked, and its own privacy policy applies from there.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission only when you first start voice input, on devices that support Apple Intelligence. Speech is transcribed on your device (the app requires on-device recognition), and your request is interpreted by Apple's on-device language model. Audio and transcripts are not recorded, stored or sent to me or to any server. You can turn these permissions off at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reading reminder, it is scheduled locally on your iPhone. There are no push notifications and no notification server. You can switch the reminder off in the app's settings or in iOS Settings.

Apple's services

Downloading the app, App Store purchases and reviews, and any crash reports or diagnostics you choose to share with developers through iOS settings are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you share diagnostics, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports, which I use only to fix bugs.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions about privacy? Email shayneptorres@gmail.com.
